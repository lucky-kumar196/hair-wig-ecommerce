# SRJ Websto – Hair Wig Website Template

A modern, elegant, and fully responsive website template designed for businesses dealing in ladies' hair wigs, human hair extensions, hair toppers, and hair replacement solutions.

This project is developed and presented by **SRJ Websto**.

## Template Overview

This website template provides a professional online presence for hair wig businesses. Its clean layout and user-friendly design help visitors explore products, understand available services, and contact the business easily.

## Template Features

- Modern and attractive homepage design
- Responsive layout for mobile, tablet, and desktop
- Hair wig and hair extension product showcases
- Product categories and collections
- Promotional sections and banners
- About and business information sections
- Contact and enquiry sections
- WhatsApp and phone contact integration
- User-friendly navigation
- Clean and professional UI design

## Template Purpose

This template is suitable for:

- Ladies' hair wig businesses
- Human hair wig sellers
- Hair extension businesses
- Hair replacement service providers
- Hair topper and hair patch businesses
- Beauty and hair solution websites
